{#- The refresh status and the Refresh now button. It is the whole content of `#refresh-area` on Settings and on the Profiles pages, and the answer to POST /refresh and GET /refresh/status, which swap it as outerHTML. The root keeps its id in every state so a swap always finds its target. Context: `refresh` (a RefreshStatus: `state`, `busy`, the newest refresh `job` and its `run`, the counts `updated` and `failed`, and `failures`, the profiles that couldn't be updated) and `refresh_time` (the daily refresh time, said before any refresh has run). While a refresh is queued or running it shows a progress bar (`refresh.progress`): a moving one while it waits and syncs Steam, then one that fills as the profiles are checked. The root polls for the status every two seconds, and says which state it showed (`was`) so that the poll which finds the refresh over can send the `profiles-refreshed` event. When nothing is pending it carries no polling at all. The same swap that ends the polling brings the button back: it is disabled (`disabled`, and `aria-disabled` for what reads the markup) while a refresh is pending, and `hx-disabled-elt` keeps a second click from going out before the answer comes back. The counts come from the run's per-profile outcomes, so a profile is counted once. A failed profile is listed with its message, which is written for the user.
